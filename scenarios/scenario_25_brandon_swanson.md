### HOOK (Scenes 1-3)

**Scene 1**
- Narration: "On May fourteenth, two thousand eight, nineteen-year-old Brandon Swanson drove his car into a ditch on a rural Minnesota road after a night out with friends. He was unhurt. He called his parents for a ride. They spoke on the phone for forty-seven minutes as Brandon walked through the darkness trying to find his location. Then — mid-sentence — Brandon said 'Oh shit' and the line went dead. He was never seen again. Not his body. Not his clothes. Not a single trace."

**Scene 9**
- Narration: "During the phone call, Brandon's parents kept him talking. He described walking through what sounded like grass or fields. He mentioned he could see lights ahead — what he thought was Lynd. He said he might be near a river because he heard water. He sounded frustrated but not panicked. For forty-seven minutes, everything seemed like a solvable problem — a kid lost in the dark, walking toward lights."

**Scene 10**
- Narration: "Then — at approximately two-thirty AM — Brandon exclaimed 'Oh shit' and the call disconnected. His parents immediately called back. No answer. They called again and again.
