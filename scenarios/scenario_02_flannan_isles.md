All three keepers had vanished without a trace."

### CLIMAX (Scenes 16-22)

**Scene 16**
- Narration: "The Northern Lighthouse Board conducted an official investigation. Superintendent Robert Muirhead concluded that all three men had gone to the western platform — likely to secure equipment during a sudden storm — and were swept away by an enormous wave. It was a rational explanation. But it had serious problems."
- Image Prompt: "Victorian-era office with a superintendent writing at a large wooden desk, lighthouse blueprints on the wall, official documents and a seal visible, oil lamp on the desk, serious atmosphere, cinematic photorealistic, 4K"
- Subtitle: "OFFICIAL CONCLUSION: SWEPT AWAY BY A MASSIVE WAVE"
- Ken Burns: Slow zoom in on the official writing

**Scene 17**
- Narration: "Problem one: lighthouse regulations strictly forbade all three keepers from leaving the building simultaneously. One man must always remain with the light. James Ducat was a twenty-year veteran — he would never have broken this rule unless circumstances were extraordinary. What could override decades of training?"

- Subtitle: "RULE: ONE KEEPER MUST ALWAYS REMAIN WITH THE LIGHT"

**Scene 18**
- Narration: "Problem two: Donald MacArthur left without his oilskins. This means he either rushed out in a panic to join the others — or he was the last one in the building when something happened that made him abandon protocol. Either way, something unprecedented occurred that broke every rule these men lived by."
- Image Prompt: "A lighthouse keeper's oilskin coat hanging alone on a hook, rain visible through an open doorway beyond, the impression of someone having just left in a hurry, storm visible outside, cinematic photorealistic, 4K"
- Subtitle: "MACARTHUR LEFT WITHOUT PROTECTION — UNPRECEDENTED"
