### HOOK (Scenes 1-3)

**Scene 1**
- Narration: "On August twentieth, nineteen sixty-six, two electronic technicians were found dead on a hillside in Niterói, Brazil. They wore formal suits. Their eyes were covered with crude lead masks. Beside them lay a notebook with cryptic instructions. There were no signs of violence. No poison was ever identified. And to this day, no one knows what killed them — or what they were trying to do."

### RISING ACTION (Scenes 9-15)

**Scene 9**
- Narration: "Beside the bodies, investigators found a small notebook. Inside was a handwritten list of instructions — a sequence of actions the two men apparently intended to follow. The note read: 'Sixteen-thirty — be at the specified location. Eighteen-thirty — ingest capsules, after the effect protect metals. Wait for the signal — the mask.'"

- Subtitle: "'INGEST CAPSULES — PROTECT METALS — WAIT FOR THE SIGNAL'"

**Scene 10**
- Narration: "The instructions implied a planned sequence: arrive at a specific time, take some kind of substance, put on the lead masks, and wait for a 'signal.' The men had followed these instructions methodically. But who wrote them? Were they following someone else's directions — or their own experimental protocol? And what was the 'signal' they were waiting for?"

The masks were shaped to cover only the eyes, as if protecting them from some kind of light or radiation that the men expected to encounter. They had been cut and shaped in advance at their electronics workshop. This was premeditated."

### CLIMAX (Scenes 16-22)

**Scene 16**
- Narration: "The night of August seventeenth — the night the men were believed to have died — several residents near Vintem Hill reported seeing strange lights in the sky. Multiple witnesses independently described blue and orange luminous objects hovering above the hillside. The reports were filed with police but never formally investigated in connection with the deaths."

- Subtitle: "WITNESSES REPORTED STRANGE LIGHTS ABOVE THE HILL"

**Scene 17**
- Narration: "Were the lights connected to the men's deaths? The timing is extraordinary. The men went to that hill expecting something — they had instructions to 'wait for the signal.' Multiple independent witnesses saw anomalous lights above that exact hill on that exact night. Coincidence becomes increasingly difficult to accept."
