Over the next six minutes, Valentich described a large object with four bright lights that circled his plane, hovered, and moved at speeds impossible for any known aircraft. His final words: 'It is not an aircraft.' Then — seventeen seconds of metallic scraping sound. Then silence. Frederick Valentich was never seen again."
