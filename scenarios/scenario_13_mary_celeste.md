### RISING ACTION (Scenes 9-15)

**Scene 9**
- Narration: "The main cabin where Captain Briggs and his family lived was orderly. The bed was unmade but not disturbed. Sarah Briggs's melodeon — a small organ — was in its place. The child's toys were undisturbed. The ship's chronometer and sextant were missing — taken by whoever left. **Scene 10**
- Narration: "The cargo hold contained seventeen hundred barrels of commercial alcohol. Upon inspection, nine barrels were found empty. The barrels were made of red oak — a porous wood — while the rest were white oak. Red oak allows vapor to seep through. It is possible — and this would become the leading theory — that these nine barrels leaked alcohol vapor into the hold, creating an invisible but highly flammable atmosphere."

A flash that burned nothing but terrified everyone aboard. Captain Briggs, experienced but cautious, ordered everyone into the lifeboat immediately."
